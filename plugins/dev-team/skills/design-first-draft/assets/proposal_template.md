## Background

The current experience or architecture, who has this problem, and what the problem actually is.
Open with: "What is the problem you're trying to solve?" Follow up on: who experiences it and how
often, what today's experience/architecture looks like, and what evidence supports it. Supporting
data (usage numbers, support volume, prior research) is welcome but optional — omit if there is
none. This section is fact, not opinion — clarify to get it right, but don't challenge it or offer
alternatives to what already exists.

## Proposed Solution

The solution at a high level — a paragraph or bulleted list of what changes, not how it's built.
Open with: "What's your proposed approach?" Push for the observable difference: what does this
look like from the outside, to the people who have the problem above? Include:

- **Goals:** what this proposal must achieve
- **Non-Goals:** what it deliberately does not address

## Success Metrics

What needle is this trying to move? Open with: "How will you know this worked?" Prefer metrics
that are observable after shipping, not proxies like "the team feels good about the approach."

## Alternatives Considered

Other approaches considered — including prior art or existing solutions found during research —
and why they weren't chosen. Open with: "What else did you consider, and why not that instead?"
If the user hasn't considered alternatives yet, this is a place to push back and propose one or
two plausible ones before accepting "none."
